# Returns

Example store policy. The store owner sets the real values in `configs/settings.dev.yaml`.

## Refund window

You can ask for a refund within 14 days of delivery. The item should be unused and in its original packaging.

## Late deliveries

If your order arrives more than 5 days after the estimated delivery date, or is still not delivered more than 5 days after that date, you can ask for a refund even if the 14-day window has passed.

## Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, send a photo or a short description of the damage. A damage claim is always reviewed by a manager. You can ask for a refund for a damaged item even after the 14-day window.

## Cash on delivery refunds

Orders paid by cash on delivery are refunded by our staff, not automatically. We will ask for your payout details through a secure channel. Please never send bank details in an email or chat message.

## Refund approval limits

Eligible prepaid refunds up to PKR 3,000 are approved automatically. Refunds from PKR 3,001 to PKR 15,000 are approved by a manager. Larger amounts, and unusual cases such as repeated refund requests, are reviewed by the store owner.

## Already refunded orders

An order cannot be refunded more than the amount paid minus any earlier refunds. Only one refund request can be open for an order at a time.

## Non-refundable items

Some categories cannot be refunded, for example gift cards and personalised items. These are marked on the product page.
