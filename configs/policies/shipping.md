# Shipping

Example store policy. The store owner sets the real values.

## Delivery times

Orders to Lahore, Karachi and other major cities usually arrive in 3 to 5 working days. Other cities take 5 to 8 working days. The estimated delivery date is shown in your order confirmation. An order is not considered late before that date.

## Tracking

Every shipped order has a tracking number. You can ask for the latest courier status at any time. If the courier does not report a status, we will tell you that the status is unknown and check again.

## Delayed orders

If the courier is delayed, we will keep you updated. If the order is more than 5 days past the estimated date, you can ask for a refund (see Returns > Late deliveries).

## Address changes

You can change the delivery address before the order is handed to the courier. After that, please contact support and we will try to help.
