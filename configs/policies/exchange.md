# Exchange

Example store policy. The store owner sets the real values.

## Exchange window

You can ask for an exchange within 14 days of delivery. The item should be unused, with tags and in its original packaging.

## Wrong size or colour

If you ordered the wrong size or colour, you can exchange it for another size or colour of the same product, subject to stock. The delivery cost of the exchange is paid by the customer.

## Wrong item sent

If we sent you a different item from the one you ordered, tell us as soon as possible. You can choose a replacement or a refund. We pay the delivery cost both ways.

## Exchange or refund

If the replacement is out of stock, you can choose a refund instead (see Returns > Refund window).
